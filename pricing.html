Pricing questions

What happens when a feature is locked on our plan?

You'll see a locked page in the navigation instead of a broken one — clicking it explains what the feature does and takes you to upgrade. Nothing fails silently.

What happens when we run out of seats?

New staff invites are blocked until you either deactivate an existing seat to free one up, or upgrade to a plan with more seats.

Can we downgrade later?

Yes, from Admin → Subscription. Downgrading may re‑lock features tied to the higher plan, so anything actively in use is worth reviewing first.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Signup assigns the free plan automatically with no payment step. You only pay when you choose to upgrade.

Do you offer pricing per hospital group?

Yes — Enterprise plans are set up for multi‑branch hospital groups and diagnostic chains. Talk to us for a plan that matches your number of branches and seats.
