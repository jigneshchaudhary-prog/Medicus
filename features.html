Home / Features

Everything your hospital does, built for the person doing it.

No shared dashboard with every switch turned on. Each role gets exactly the screens their job needs — here's what each one actually looks like day to day.

Front Desk

The first and last touchpoint of every visit — registration, the queue, and billing.

A day at the front desk

From the moment a patient walks in to the moment they're billed, front desk owns the handoffs — and Medicus keeps every step in one board instead of a notebook, a call register and a separate billing app.

1

Register

Name, age, gender, phone. New patients can go through WhatsApp OTP, or be registered without OTP when needed.

2

Book or walk in

Pull an open slot that respects the doctor's schedule and leave, or walk the patient straight into the next free slot.

3

Work the queue

Booked → Arrived → On‑going → Reviewed, on a shared board doctors see too. Cancel or mark no‑show without losing the slot's history.

4

Bill

Create the invoice straight from the appointment's items, collect cash or bank payment, and send the PDF over WhatsApp.

Token numbers that mean something

Tokens are issued once, at check‑in, per doctor per calendar day. Any number shown before that is a preview only.

Discount ceilings

Front desk discounting is capped by policy; admin isn't limited the same way — so pricing stays controlled at the point of sale.

IPD admit desk

Admit a patient, allocate a bed, and schedule the doctor's first bedside visit — all from the same desk that handles OPD.

Discharge paperwork

Handle discharge documentation from the admission page itself, once billing is clear.

Doctor

Consults, the Visit Pad, lab orders, bedside visits and referrals — one queue to work through.

A day of consults

A doctor's dashboard opens on "my day" — the queue, pending tasks, and any inpatient visits due. Owner-doctors also get Operations and Finance views without leaving their login.

1

Open the queue

See who's Arrived and next, alongside any IPD bedside visits scheduled for today.

2

Write the Visit Pad

Diagnosis, medicines, lab orders, diagrams and attachments — saved as a draft until you're ready to commit.

3

Finalise the Rx

Finalising moves the visit to Reviewed. Need to change something after? Amend it — the prior version is kept, not overwritten.

4

Refer, if needed

Bring in another doctor live over video, or send an offline referral — a WhatsApp Rx snapshot plus the referred doctor's contact details.

Add patient = walk‑in

A doctor adding a patient directly creates a walk‑in appointment for themselves — no separate "new patient" flow to learn.

Final Rx only, in lists

Consults and prescriptions lists only ever show the finalised record — drafts stay out of the way until you're done.

Lab ordering and review

Order labs straight from the Visit Pad and review results as they come back, without switching systems.

IPD bedside visits

Complete a scheduled bedside visit and write a prescription linked to that specific visit and admission.

Invite to Medicus

A personal referral link lets a doctor invite peers or their own second clinic onto the platform.

Works offline

Adding a patient, running a consult and writing a prescription keep working without a connection, and sync once you're back online.

Admin

Runs the hospital's operations — staff, pricing, branding, data — and can step into any other view.

Running the hospital, not just the software

Admin owns everything that keeps the hospital operating correctly behind the scenes, and can switch into Doctor or Front Desk view directly — the setup most small and mid‑sized hospitals actually use.

Staff & seats

Invite staff by email set‑password or a temporary password. Seats are capped by your plan; deactivating a staff member frees one back up.

Masters → Services

The single price list — fee, tax and billing category per service. Doctors never set their own fees; pricing stays consistent hospital‑wide.

Branding & Rx layout

Letterhead, prescription layout, tax settings, working hours and your WhatsApp sender identity, all from Settings.

ABDM & HFR

Configure your Health Facility Registry ID and ABDM settings as your hospital moves onto India's national health interchange.

Data Hub

Import existing patient records from Excel or CSV — with optional automatic column classification — and export any time.

Support, direct to Medicus

Support tickets go straight to the Medicus platform team, not a shared inbox.

Super Admin

The platform layer — for whoever runs Medicus itself, or a group operating many hospitals.

Commercial visibility, without clinical access

Super Admin sees the business of running a hospital platform — tenants, plans, revenue — and is structurally kept out of patient data unless a specific, audited session is opened.

Tenant management

Create, suspend, or open any hospital tenant on the platform.

Plans & subscriptions

Set locked pages, seat counts and free/paid status per plan, and manage each hospital's subscription and invoices.

Audited impersonation

Step into a hospital's account for up to 60 minutes to help directly — every session and every write is logged.

Platform dashboard

MRR, ARR, plan mix and collections across every hospital — commercial KPIs only, never patient records.

Underneath, the platform basics are covered

Realtime by default

Queue and appointment changes push out live, so front desk and doctor screens never fall out of sync.

Works through a dropped connection

Adding a patient, consulting, prescribing and billing all queue locally and sync automatically once you're back online.

Consistent dates, everywhere

Every screen displays dates DD/MM/YYYY; every filter reads your local day, not UTC midnight.

Clear status colours

Booked, Arrived, Reviewed, Paid — the same fixed colours across every board, so status is legible at a glance.
