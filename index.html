Hard rule: One role → One UI surface.

Every desk logs into the surface built for what they actually do. (Admin includes a fast-switcher for all hospital views).

Doctor Workspace

Runs the day's queue, writes diagnosis on the Visit Pad, and executes IPD bedside visits.

  • Visit Pad (Draft & Finalise Rx)
  • Lab ordering & result review
  • Refer a Doctor (LiveKit Video)

Front Desk

Registers patients, walks them in, manages the queue board, and handles all OPD/IPD billing.

  • Tokens assigned ONLY on create
  • Queue: Booked → Arrived → On-going
  • IPD Admit, allocate bed, schedule

Hospital Admin

The hospital owner/ops view. Manage seats, services, and jump into clinical roles.

  • Admin | Doctor | Frontdesk switcher
  • Masters (Single price list)
  • Data hub (Import CSV/Excel)

Platform Super Admin

Manages subscriptions and plans across the SaaS. Never sees PHI without an audited session.

  • MRR, Plan Mix, Collections
  • Tenant & Seat cap management
  • Audited impersonation sessions
