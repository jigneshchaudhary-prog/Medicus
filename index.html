Frequently asked questions

What are the seat limits on the Free plan?

The Free plan includes access for up to 3 standard users (e.g., 1 Doctor, 2 Front Desk). You can upgrade to Growth at any time to add more seats.

Are IPD features locked to specific tiers?

Yes, In-Patient Department (IPD) workflows, including bedside visits and admission tracking, are available starting on the Growth plan.

Does Medicus work offline?

Medicus is offline-tolerant. While it requires an internet connection for real-time queue syncing, active Visit Pad sessions safely cache data if the connection drops momentarily.

Can I manage multiple clinic locations?

Yes, the Enterprise tier supports multi-tenant orchestration, allowing you to manage permissions, billing, and reporting across multiple independent clinics from one Super Admin console.

Is our patient data secure and isolated?

Absolutely. Medicus relies on strict logical tenant isolation at the database level, ensuring one hospital's data can never be queried or accessed by another account.
