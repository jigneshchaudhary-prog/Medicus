Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is our clinical data visible to the Medicus platform?
No. Medicus utilizes a strict multi-tenant architecture. All clinical data (Protected Health Information) is locked behind a strict @PhiResource() guard. The platform layer can only see commercial KPIs (like total subscriptions) and cannot access patient records without an audited, 60-minute impersonation token initiated by you.
What happens if we lose internet connection during a consult?
Medicus features an Offline PWA mode. Adding a patient, running a consult, writing a prescription, and queuing bills will continue to work locally. The system will automatically sync with the cloud the moment your connection is restored.
How does billing work between OPD and IPD?
For OPD, the invoice is generated directly from the finalized appointment snapshot. For IPD, the admission carries everything (bed charges, bedside visits, labs). A patient cannot be discharged in the system if unbilled charges remain on their admission record.
Can doctors set their own consultation fees?
No. To maintain hospital-wide consistency, all pricing is controlled by the Hospital Admin via the Centralized Masters module. This ensures discounts, taxes, and billing categories remain uniform across the entire facility.
Do patients need to download a separate app?
No app download is required. We utilize direct WhatsApp integration for sending OTPs, booking confirmations, and delivering Prescription/Invoice PDFs directly to the patient's phone.
